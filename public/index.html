Content Creator Quick Reference Manual

1. Image Naming Cheat Sheet (Automatic Timeline Sync)

Prefixing filenames with timestamps gives you instant, frame-perfect synchronization with zero manual dragging:

Filename Example Timeline Position Use Case
0-00.png / 0-03.png Starts at 00:00 or 00:03 Intro hook / Title scene
0-15.png / 0-28.jpg Starts at 00:15 or 00:28 Story development / Key points
1-05.png / 2-20.webp Starts at 01:05 or 02:20 Longer multi-minute narratives
scene_01.png, scene_02.png Evenly Auto-Paced (~4.5s) Sequential storyboard scenes

2. Recommended Video Formats & Dimensions

16:9 Landscape (1920x1080 or 4K) Ideal for standard YouTube, documentaries, video essays, and desktop viewing.
9:16 Vertical (1080x1920) Ideal for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok videos.
PNG vs JPG vs WebP PNG is best for images with text and line art; JPG and WebP are best for photographic realism.

3. Audio & Smart Music Ducking

When you enable Background Music with Dynamic Ducking, the audio engine automatically lowers the background music volume by -18 dB whenever speech is present, ensuring your voiceover remains crisp, clear, and professional without manual volume automation.
